Programmes

Four areas of support, from Grade 8 to Grade 12

Career Compass SA offers a comprehensive support programme consisting of four key areas.

1. All grades

Career Guidance

Helping learners explore what's out there and make sense of it, at any stage of high school.

  • Career exploration and decision-making
  • Understanding requirements for different careers
  • Exposure to high-demand and relevant careers
  • Planning post-matric pathways
2. Grade 9 learners

Subject Choice Guidance

Grade 9 is where a career actually starts to take shape — the subjects chosen here open or close doors in Grade 10 and beyond.

  • Importance of subject selection for Grade 10
  • Understanding career pathways and subject requirements
  • Guidance on choosing appropriate streams (Science, Commerce, Humanities)
  • Avoiding common mistakes when selecting subjects
3. Grade 12 learners

University & TVET Applications

Applications are time-sensitive and paperwork-heavy — this is where late or incorrect submissions most often cost learners a place.

  • Step-by-step application processes
  • CAO and direct university applications
  • Required documentation
  • Application deadlines
  • NSFAS and bursary guidance
4. Grades 8–12

Academic Support (Tutoring)

Guidance means little without the marks to back it up — our tutoring keeps learners on track for the subjects they've chosen.

  • Tutoring in Mathematics and Physical Sciences (more subjects to be added along the way)
  • Support with challenging concepts
  • Exam preparation and revision
  • Improving learner performance

Programme delivery

How sessions are run, and who they're for.

Target groups

Grade 9 learners (subject choices), Grade 12 learners (career guidance & applications), and tutoring for all grades (8–12).

Format

Interactive PowerPoint presentations, 45–60 minutes per session, with real-life examples and Q&A.

Method

Presentations, learner engagement, question & answer sessions, and real-life examples throughout.
