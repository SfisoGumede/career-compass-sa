For Schools

A free programme that supports your learners and your staff

Career Compass SA works alongside schools to deliver career guidance and subject choice sessions — at no cost, on your schedule.

Book a session

What this programme does for your school

  • Helps Grade 9 learners choose correct subject streams
  • Improves learners' career awareness
  • Increases successful university and TVET applications
  • Reduces late or incorrect applications
  • Improves academic performance through tutoring
  • Supports the Life Orientation curriculum
  • Prepares learners for life after matric

How a session works

  1. Target group is confirmed

    Sessions are built for Grade 9 learners (subject choices) or Grade 12 learners (career guidance & applications). Tutoring is available for all grades, 8–12.

  2. Session is scheduled

    Each session runs 45–60 minutes, arranged around your school's timetable.

  3. Interactive delivery

    Delivered as an interactive PowerPoint presentation, with real-life examples and open Q&A throughout.

  4. Learners walk away with a plan

    Learners leave with a clearer sense of subject choices, career pathways, or application steps — depending on the session.

Cost

Free

Career guidance & subject choice

Free of charge, offered on a voluntary basis. The main objective is to create impact and support learners within the community.

Priced per session

Tutoring & school partnerships

Tutoring is priced per session (see Tutoring & Pricing). Wider school partnership programmes — after-school classes, Saturday classes, holiday revision — are structured and priced around your school's needs.
