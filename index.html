Helping learners to make informed decisions on their Careers.

Career Compass SA guides high school learners through subject choices, career decisions, university & TVET applications, and the schoolwork that gets them there - with free guidance and affordable tutoring.

What we help with

  • Choosing the right subjects before Grade 10
  • Understanding careers and what they require
  • CAO, university & TVET applications for Grade 12s
  • NSFAS and bursary guidance
  • Tutoring in Maths & Physical Sciences

Four areas of support, one goal

From choosing a subject stream in Grade 9 to submitting a university application in Grade 12, we walk alongside learners at every stage.

All grades

Career Guidance

Explore careers, understand what they require, and plan a realistic post-matric pathway.

Grade 9

Subject Choice Guidance

Choose the right stream — Science, Commerce or Humanities — with your future career in mind.

Grade 12

University & TVET Applications

Step-by-step help with CAO, direct applications, documentation, deadlines, NSFAS and bursaries.

Grades 8–12

Academic Support

Tutoring in Mathematics and Physical Sciences, with exam prep and revision support.
